Zone Schema\n\nDefined in `server/src/models/Zone.ts`:\n\n- `zoneId` (String, unique, indexed): Formatted as `ZONE-YYMMDD-XXXX`.\n- `name` (String, unique, indexed): Sanitized zone name.\n- `category` (String, enum): `PROTECTED_FOREST` | `RESERVED_AREA` | `COMMERCIAL_LEASE` | `SUSPICIOUS_HOTSPOT`.\n- `status` (String, enum): `PROTECTED` (Active) | `SUSPICIOUS` (Attention) | `SUSPENDED` (Deactivated).\n- `boundary` (GeoJSON Polygon): Polygon coords `[longitude, latitude]` with a `2dsphere` index.\n- `centerPoint` (GeoJSON Point): Center point `[longitude, latitude]` with a `2dsphere` index.\n- `baselineDate` (Date): Satellite image acquisition date.\n- `baselineImageUrl` / `baselineImageRef` (String): Satellite visual resource indicators.\n- `createdBy` (ObjectId): Creator profile trace pointing to `User` collection.\n\n---\n\n## 3. APIs Implemented\n\n| Endpoint | HTTP Method | Required Permission | Action |\n|---|---|---|---|\n| `/api/zones` | `GET` | `AREA_READ` | Search, filter, and list monitored zones |\n| `/api/zones/:id` | `GET` | `AREA_READ` | Fetch detail data for one zone |\n| `/api/zones` | `POST` | `AREA_CREATE` | Validate GeoJSON, compute center, and create zone |\n| `/api/zones/:id` | `PATCH` | `AREA_UPDATE` | Edit name, category, status, and redraw polygon |\n| `/api/zones/:id/suspend` | `PATCH` | `AREA_UPDATE` | Set zone status to `SUSPENDED` (soft deactivation) |\n\n---\n\n## 4. Map Functionality\n- **Map Container**: Centered dynamically around Tamil Nadu context (`[11.1271, 78.6569]`).\n- **Zone Display**: Renders existing zones as polygons. Fill and border colors distinguish categories and statuses.\n- **Custom Polygon Drawing**: Click-to-draw vertex system. Click markers to place, undo points dynamically, and preview polygons. Closed polygons are generated back to the first point.\n- **Table-Map Synchronization**:\n  - Selecting a zone in the table triggers map pan-and-zoom actions.\n  - Clicking on a map polygon opens its details in the right-hand drawer panel.\n  - Creating or editing areas immediately updates both elements without reloading.\n\n---\n\n## 5.
