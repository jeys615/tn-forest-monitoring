n\n## 7. Manual User Workflow for the First Real Monitoring Test\n\n1. **Log in to UI**:\n   - Navigate to `http://localhost:5173/login`.\n   - Email: `admin@tnforest.gov.in` | Password: `Admin@TNFMS2026!`\n2. **Create a Real Monitored Zone**:\n   - Go to **Areas / Monitored Zones** (`/areas`).\n   - Click **Draw Zone** or enter your custom polygon coordinates.\n   - Set the zone category (e.g., `RESERVED_AREA`, `QUARRY_SITE`, or `MINING_ZONE`).\n   - Save the zone.\n3. **Register / Activate Baseline**:\n   - Go to **Baselines** or select the newly created zone.\n   - Choose or query the baseline satellite image (e.g. Sentinel-2 from an earlier date) and set status to **ACTIVE**.\n4. **Trigger Real Monitoring Job**:\n   - Go to **Monitoring** (`/monitoring`).\n   - Click **Schedule Job** / **Run Monitoring Now**.\n   - Select your newly created zone.\n   - Click **Trigger Processing**.\n\n---\n\n## 8.
